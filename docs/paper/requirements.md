## 80+ Mark Band — Full Grid Summary 80分以上标准综述

Source: `marking_criteria_-_standard_grid_101_24-25.pdf`. 8 weighted criteria = 100%. For each: 80+ requirement, and the 70-79→80+ delta (what word/phrase marks the jump), plus which chapter it maps to.
来源:`marking_criteria_-_standard_grid_101_24-25.pdf`。8 项加权标准合计100%。逐项给出:80+ 要求、70-79→80+ 的分水岭措辞、对应章节。

| # | Criterion 标准 | Weight | 80+ requires 80+要求 | 70-79→80+ delta 分水岭 | Maps to 对应章节 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Formulate RQ & objectives 拟定RQ与目标 | 10% | Well-argued RQ; gap in knowledge identified; outcomes expressly articulated; work "perfectly scoped...balanced" 论证充分的RQ;明确识别知识缺口;成果明确阐述;工作完美平衡地规划 | Gap must be **explicitly named**, not implied; scoping goes from "achievable" to "perfect/balanced" 缺口须明说;规划从"可完成"升级为"完美平衡" | Introduction §2 |
| 2 | Achievement of objectives 目标达成 | 10% | "Irrefutably achieved"; **no** evidence of incomplete work 无可辩驳地达成;**零**未完成迹象 | "significant evidence" → "**any** evidence" 从"没有明显迹象"到"没有任何迹象" | Conclusion |
| 3 | Critical evaluation of literature 文献批判评价 | **20%** | "Exhaustive" critical analysis, "outstandingly skilful" synthesis; source reliability "addressed **fully** in analysis" 详尽的批判分析;极娴熟的综合;可信度在分析中被充分论证 | "comprehensive/skilful" → "exhaustive/outstandingly skilful"; reliability "addressed" → "addressed fully" 程度副词升级;可信度从"提及"到"充分论证" | Literature Review |
| 4 | Research methods selection & justification 方法选择论证 | **20%** | "Full...selection **and application**" via "**rigorous**" methodology; "**substantive**" risk consideration/mitigation 全面的选择**与运用**,方法论严谨;风险考虑与缓解**实质性** | Adds "rigorous" as a quality dimension; "comprehensive" risk → "substantive" risk 增加"严谨性"维度;风险考虑从"全面"到"实质性" | Methodology |
| 5 | Investigation informed by prior work 调查以文献为据 | 10% | "**Complete**" conceptual understanding, "**outstanding**" technical competence; "exhaustive" analysis 完整的概念理解,卓越的技术能力,详尽的分析 | "near complete" → "complete" (no remaining gaps allowed) 从"近乎完整"到"完整"——不允许剩余缺口 | Methodology + Results/Analysis |
| 6 | Present findings + evaluate confidence 清晰呈现+评估可信度 | 10% | Suitable for a **peer-reviewed journal** (minor revisions); conclusions "fully justified"; recommendations "practical, **detailed**, convincing" with **clear** resource consideration 达到同行评审期刊发表水准;结论完全得到论证;建议实用详细有说服力,资源考虑清楚明确 | Publishable venue: conference (70-79) → journal (80+); recommendations gain "detailed" + "clear indication" 可发表层级从会议升级为期刊;建议增加"详细"与"清楚表明" | Results/Analysis + Discussion |
| 7 | Demonstration of benefit 成果价值 | 10% | "**Undeniably**" illustrates benefit/worth; "**total** mastery" of subject 无可否认地展示价值;完全掌握学科 | "convincingly" → "undeniably"; "mastery" → "total mastery" 程度副词升级到顶格 | Discussion |
| 8 | Presentation of dissertation 论文呈现 | 10% | "Outstanding" heading/structure use; "**all** points" understood in one reading; "**faultless**" grammar; "few or no errors" in diagrams/contents/refs 标题结构运用卓越;一遍读懂**所有**要点;语法无懈可击;图表目录引用少有或无错误 | "most points" → "all points"; "few errors" → "faultless" 从"大部分要点"到"所有要点";从"少量错误"到"无差错" | Whole document 全文 |

**Cross-cutting pattern 跨标准规律:** the 70-79→80+ jump is almost never a new task — it's closing every remaining gap in what's already being done ("comprehensive"→"exhaustive", "most"→"all", "convincingly"→"undeniably"). **80+ is a completeness standard, not a scope standard**: every checklist item below needs zero exceptions, not just average satisfaction.
70-79→80+ 的跃升几乎不是新增任务,而是把已经在做的事情的剩余缺口全部补齐。**80+ 是完整性标准,不是覆盖范围标准**——下文每个 checklist 项都要零例外满足,而非平均满足。

> **✅ 项目专属提醒(已更正):** `thesis-chapter-outline.md` 自称总字数"13,300 字",但这是源文件自身的算术错误——八段字数(1,300+2,800+3,600+2,600+2,000+1,600+900+700)实际相加是 **15,500**,已经落在容差区间内,不需要大幅调整。各章具体字数预算见下文对应章节。(建议同时把 `thesis-chapter-outline.md` 里的算式也改正。)

### Included in word count 计入字数
- Main text: Introduction → Conclusion 正文全部章节
- Quotations 引语
- **Tables and figures (including text inside them)** 表格、图示(含其中文字)
- Footnotes, endnotes 脚注、尾注
- In-text citations 文中引用
- Main project title, all headings/titles 主标题及各级标题
- **Abstract / summary** 摘要

### Excluded from word count 不计入字数
- Table of Contents, List of Figures, List of Tables 目录、图表清单
- Declaration page 声明页
- Acknowledgements page 致谢页
- Reference List / Bibliography 参考文献列表
- **All material in an Appendix/Annex** 附录/附件中的全部材料

### Hard rule 硬性规则
**"Text within an image is not permitted."** 图片里不允许嵌入文字——架构图/流程图里需要的文字标签必须用可编辑的原生 Word 元素(文本框、SmartArt、表格)呈现,不能是"画在图片里烧录进去"的文字(即便这类文字理论上不计入字数,也不允许这样做)。所有实质内容都要以真实文字存在,不能靠图片规避字数统计。

### Sanctions for exceeding word count 超字数处罚(阶梯式)
| Excess 超出幅度 | Sanction 处罚 |
|---|---|
| ≤10% | None (lenience) 无(容差范围内) |
| 10%–30% | −10 percentage points from total mark, capped at pass mark 总分扣10个百分点,但不低于及格线 |
| >30% | Mark capped at pass mark 总分直接封顶到及格线 |

## Introduction 引言

**Marking criterion:** "Formulate a research question and derived objectives suitable for the degree and consistent with the time and resource available to conduct the research."
*拟定与学位水平相符、与可用时间/资源一致的研究问题及衍生目标。*

**Word budget 字数预算:** 1,300 words (`thesis-chapter-outline.md` §1); could take +100 for expanded RQ-consolidation argumentation in §2.
**字数预算:** 1,300 字(对应 `thesis-chapter-outline.md` §1);§2 因RQ整合需要更多论证空间,可考虑+100字。

### 1. Background 研究背景
- [ ] Overview of research area, credible sources 研究领域概述+可信来源
- [ ] Specific problem identified + justified 具体问题+论证重要性
- [ ] Knowledge gap identified + justified 知识缺口+论证重要性

结构:漏斗——领域→问题→缺口,逐步收窄。

### 2. Aim, question & objectives 目的、问题与目标

- [ ] **One** RQ, project-wide scope 一个RQ,覆盖全项目
- [ ] **4–5** SMART objectives, each justified 4–5个SMART目标,各自论证

> **项目备注:** 现有草稿有三个 RQ(RQ1–3),需整合为一个 RQ + 4–5 个 SMART objectives。

### 3. Expected outcomes 预期成果
- [ ] Practice impact + beneficiary + justified 实践影响+受益方+论证
- [ ] Academic impact (gap filled) + justified 学术影响(填补缺口)+论证
- [ ] Must match Discussion/Conclusion delivery 须与Discussion/Conclusion实际交付一致

### 4. Structure of dissertation 论文结构
- [ ] Chapters outlined, objectives mapped to chapters 章节概述+目标对应
- [ ] Visual (if any): captioned, referenced before it appears 图示(如有):有标题,正文先提及

## Literature Review 文献综述

**Marking criterion:** "Critically evaluate the context of the research, synthesising ideas from a referenced review of relevant source material."
*批判性评价研究背景,综合引用相关文献资料的观点。*

跨章节关联:本章是 Methodology/Results 标准"以前人研究为据"的证据来源,须与后续章节呼应。结构:倒金字塔——领域→问题→缺口。

**Word budget 字数预算:** 2,800 words (`Related Work` in outline) — one of the two 20%-weighted criteria; protect this allocation rather than trimming it to free up room elsewhere.
**字数预算:** 2,800 字(对应大纲 `Related Work`)——占两个20%权重最高标准之一,应优先保护这个预算,不要为了给别的章节腾字数而压缩它。

### 1. Introduction 引言
- [ ] What/why/how of the chapter 讲什么/为什么/怎么组织
- [ ] Aligned to objective(s) 对齐目标

### 2. Each section 各小节
- [ ] Credible, relevant sources 可信相关来源
- [ ] **Synthesise**, not describe(核心动作是综合,非描述)
- [ ] Critically evaluate sources 批判性评价来源
- [ ] Quotes/paraphrase: Harvard cited, used sparingly 引用/转述:Harvard格式,少用
- [ ] Synthesis mode: every source in Reference List 综合模式:来源均入列表
- [ ] Non-English sources: original + own translation 非英文来源:原文+翻译
- [ ] Section-to-section flow (link prev/next) 节间衔接前后

### 3. Conclusion 结论
- [ ] Summary + gap restated 要点小结+重申缺口
- [ ] Purpose achieved, linked to next chapter 目的达成+承接下一章

## Methodology 方法论

**Marking criterion:** "Select and justify choice of approach taken in research...to suit the requirements of the specific research question and to consider risks...applying suitable mitigations."
*选择并论证研究方法,契合具体研究问题,妥善考虑风险并给出缓解措施。*

每个方法层级小节都遵循:**描述 → 论证 → 评估**(含局限及应对)。

**Word budget 字数预算:** 6,200 words total = 3,600 (`System and Methodology`) + 2,600 (`Data Collection and Analysis Plan`, maps to §5 below) — the other 20%-weighted criterion; the largest chapter in the dissertation, appropriately so.
**字数预算:** 合计 6,200 字 = 3,600(`System and Methodology`)+ 2,600(`Data Collection and Analysis Plan`,对应下文§5)——另一个20%权重最高标准,是全文最大的一章,分配合理。

### 1. Introduction 引言
- [ ] Restate RQ/objectives, overview design, chapter structure 重述RQ/目标,概述设计,本章结构

### 2. Philosophical paradigm 哲学范式
- [ ] Epistemology + ontology identified & justified 认识论+本体论,均论证
- [ ] Paradigm's implications discussed(影响方法选择/分析/贡献可推广性)

### 3. Design and logic 设计与逻辑
- [ ] Qual/quant choice for collection & analysis, justified 收集与分析的定性/定量选择,论证
- [ ] Single/multi/mixed method justified 单一/多元/混合方法论证
- [ ] Reasoning type (inductive/deductive) 推理类型
- [ ] Sample size calculated & justified 样本量+论证
- [ ] Data/participant access described (barriers) 数据获取(含障碍)
- [ ] Subject analysis type identified 研究对象分析类型

### 4. Method(s) for data collection 数据收集方法
- [ ] Each method: described, justified, evaluated 每种方法:描述+论证+评估
- [ ] Tools/software: university-approved, evaluated 工具/软件:获批+评估
- [ ] Branch detail as applicable (participants / materials / experiment-model / lit-review) 按适用分支补充细节
- [ ] Detailed enough to replicate 细节足以复现

> **项目备注:** 本项目适用**实验/仿真/模型**分支(agent版本、prompt模板、沙盒环境、baseline校验、度量设计假设),参与者/材料分支不适用。

### 5. Method(s) for data analysis 数据分析方法
- [ ] Each technique: described, justified, evaluated 每种技术:描述+论证+评估
- [ ] Type-specific detail (equations/variables/tests/themes) 按类型补充细节

### 6. Ethical implications 伦理影响
- [ ] Approval process stated (e.g. WMG delegated approval) 说明审批流程
- [ ] Data storage/security discussed 数据存储安全
- [ ] Appendices: approval email, ethics form, training certs 附录:批准邮件/表格/培训证明

### 7. Conclusion 结论
- [ ] Purpose + achievement, linked forward 目的达成+承接下一章

> **项目备注:** §2 范式大概率是(后)实证主义,需正文明确论证;§6 虽无人类被试,仍需说明走的审批路径。

*Source: Methodology chapter guidance + marking grid (80+ requires full selection AND application of tools).*

## Results and Analysis 结果与分析

**核心区分:** Results = 发现了什么;Analysis = 意味着什么(须回扣文献,产出RQ答案)。结构可合可分,不确定时问导师。

**Marking criteria:** "Devise and perform an investigation, informed by...previous workers...efficiently utilising available resources and dealing with problems appropriately." / "Present findings...with clarity, appropriately evaluating the confidence that should be placed in any findings."
*调查以前人研究为据,高效利用资源,妥善处理问题 / 清晰呈现发现,恰当评估可信度。*

**Word budget 字数预算:** 2,000 words (`Findings` in outline) — flagged as a data-dependent placeholder; revisit once trajectory runs are finalized, may need to grow.
**字数预算:** 2,000 字(对应大纲 `Findings`)——标记为数据未定的占位预算,待轨迹运行数据定稿后需重新核实,不排除要增加。

### 1. Introduction 引言
- [ ] What/why/how, aligned to objective(s) 讲什么/为什么/怎么组织,对齐目标

### 2. Results 结果
- [ ] Clear headings tied to RQ/objectives 标题对齐RQ/目标
- [ ] Logical ordering (general→specific / chronological / cause-effect) 逻辑顺序
- [ ] Past tense findings, present tense tables/figures 发现用过去时,图表用现在时
- [ ] No irrelevant content, no duplicated findings 不加无关内容,不重复呈现
- [ ] **Data authenticity maintained** — fabrication = academic misconduct **数据真实性**——造假属学术不端
- [ ] Qual: quotes anonymised/cited sparingly 定性:引语匿名化,少用
- [ ] Quant: test + p value stated; tables edited (not pasted) 定量:检验+p值;表格需编辑

### 3. Analysis 分析
- [ ] Confidence evaluated (validity/reliability or qual. equivalents) 评估可信度
- [ ] Results linked to prior research (similarities/differences/why) 与已有研究对比+原因
- [ ] Unexpected results discussed & justified 讨论意外结果
- [ ] Alternative explanations from multiple perspectives 多角度解释
- [ ] Interpretation with appropriate caution on confidence 谨慎给出解读
- [ ] Literature concepts/theories applied 运用文献概念
- [ ] Each result tied to RQ/objectives → contribution 每个结果连接RQ/目标→贡献

字数分配向 Analysis 倾斜,不要把篇幅都花在呈现结果上。

### 4. Conclusion 结论
- [ ] Summary, purpose achieved, linked forward 小结+目的达成+承接下一章

> **项目备注:** 本章定量为主,数据真实性红线尤其关键——每个数字须能追溯回 `docs/methodology/analysis.md` 的处理流程。

*Source: Results and Analysis chapter guidance + marking grid.*

*证明工作价值;建议须清楚考虑资源需求。*

**字数预算:** 合计 2,500 字 = 1,600(`Discussion`)+ 900(`Limitations`,大纲中是独立章节,对应下文§3)。

### 1. Recommendations for practice 实践建议
- [ ] Restate problem 重申问题
- [ ] Recommendations based on results analysis 基于结果分析的建议
- [ ] Stakeholder benefit (micro/macro) 利益相关方益处

### 2. Contribution to knowledge 知识贡献

### 4. Suggestions for future research 未来研究建议

### 5. Conclusion 结论
- [ ] Summary, purpose achieved, tied to RQ 小结+目的达成+呼应RQ

> **项目备注:** Recommendations 撰写时直接带上到 Results/Analysis 的交叉引用;Limitations 区分研究整体局限(单一案例系统、T1/T2/T3规模不对等等,已见 `thesis-chapter-outline.md` §7)与方法论局限,每条须配解决方案。
